Refunds

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Try before you pay

Every installation starts with a 7-day free trial, so you can check that 91 TV works with your provider and your device before you buy.

If it does not work for you

If you buy a plan and 91 TV does not work for you, reply to your receipt email within 14 days of purchase and we will refund you.

Subscriptions

Monthly and yearly plans can be cancelled at any time. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Lemon Squeezy and usually appear within a few days.
