Questions

Good to know.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new install gets 7 days free with all features. No code and no card needed. When it ends, choose a plan to keep watching.

Do you include channels or a subscription?

No. 91 TV is the player app only. You bring your own IPTV provider and add it with an M3U link or an Xtream Codes login.

Which devices does it work on?

Android TV and Google TV devices (Sony, TCL, Skyworth, Hisense, Philips, Chromecast with Google TV and more) and Windows 10 / 11 PCs. There are separate plans for TV and Windows.

How many devices can I use one code on?

Two. If you change TV or PC, open Settings › Licence on the old one and remove it, then activate the same code on the new one.

Does it work without internet?

Streaming needs internet, of course. The licence itself keeps working for 7 days without a connection.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly and yearly plans can be cancelled anytime from the link in your receipt email; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get help?

Reply to your 91 TV receipt email and we'll help you with installing, activating or moving your licence.
