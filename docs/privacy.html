What stays on your device

Your IPTV server address, username and password, your lists and what you have watched stay on your own device. We do not receive them.

What is checked online

To start the free trial and to check your activation code, the app contacts our licence service with your code and a device name. Prayer times are requested from a public prayer-times service for your city. If you add an OMDb key, ratings are requested from that service.

Payments

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy. We never see your card details. Lemon Squeezy shares your name and email with us only so that we can support your order.

Selling or sharing

We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

Your choices

You can delete the app at any time, which removes everything stored on your device. To ask about information held for your order, reply to your receipt email.
